Two Keepers - Gardens of Cyrus
==============================

Two players, one PC. Run TwoKeepers.exe. It is a single self-contained file.

KEEPER 1 (sturdy)   A / D move    SPACE jump    S act    F change shape    R restart
KEEPER 2 (swift)    arrows move   Z or UP jump  X act    C change shape    V restart
Pads                left stick    A jump        X act    Y change          START pause
Shared              ESC or P pause              BACKSPACE restart the garden

ACT is both a tap and a hold. The sturdy keeper taps to lift a partner onto his
shoulders and holds to root in place. The swift keeper taps to throw or release
the tether and holds to reel in or swing.

You can only change shape while the braid of light between the two keepers is
bright, so stay close. Every shape change is paid for out of the shared garden
well, and the well refills about three times faster when you both stand in it.

A spill sends a keeper back to the last waystone, so touching one costs seconds,
not progress.

The game uses the GL Compatibility renderer, so it runs on integrated graphics
and older machines. If it ever refuses to start, the machine's graphics driver
is the thing to update.

On first run Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unknown publisher: this build
is not code-signed. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
